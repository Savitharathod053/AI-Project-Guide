Complete End-to-End System Mechanism

The sensor stage acquires real-time physical telemetry. The microcontroller reads the electrical inputs over validated hardware buses (ADC, UART, I2C), normalizes and filters raw readings, and evaluates the project logic against set thresholds. When an actuation condition is reached, the controller safely triggers optocoupler-isolated relays or motor drivers, updates the local display, and dispatches encrypted telemetry packets to cloud dashboards.
